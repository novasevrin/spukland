Spukland

Spukland or "The Land of Haunting" is a collection of countries and realms sharing a common history under Lyre's death. Spukland can only be accessed by finding a black door in certain locations and walking through. The elusive nature of the black doors, along with certain texts originating in Spukland has come under the scrutiny of several governments and religious bodies. These often involve the remnants of the former Lyrenity and the former empire that once united much of the continent. It is doubtful we will ever fully understand the true scope of Spukland, but the mystery inspires us to venture deeper into its embrace. Spukland™ created by Nova Sevrin.

What's Das Spukland Projekt?

Das Spukland Projekt is the official name of the worldbuilding project encompassing both the world of Spukland and Apocras. The project began initially as an epic dark fantasy trilogy centered around the Sevrin family and their downfall. Today, Das Spukland Projekt is firmly a multi-media project encompassing literature, music, pixel art, animation etc. Conceived originally in May of 2023, Spukland has been years in the making.

Inspirations and Story

Of what inspired Das Spukland Projekt(DSP), a select few worth mentioning are:

  1. The German Language(linguistically.)
  2. Anthology of The Killer by thecatamites(partial art direction and theming inspiration.)
  3. Germanic and Celtic folklore elements such as Faeries and Elves(Mythbuilding.)
  4. Historic figures such as Johann Konrad Dippel and gothic literary tradition such as Frankenstein and The Castle of Otranto(Root of Imagination.)
  5. Authors and Composers such as Steven Erikson, J R R Tolkien, Sigmund Krähe, Johann Sebastian Bach, Mary Shelley, etc.(Style and inspiration for learning related skills.)

Beyond that, the story of DSP is also fundamentally rooted in my own life experience. For that reason, developing DSP is quite personal for me and I wish to share it as fully as I possibly can.

The basic story behind Spukland, also known as "Lyre's Myth," follows Lyre, the original teacher and guide of all those who dwelled in the heavens. He founded all the fields of knowledge and much like everyone else, he lived in light and possessed immortality. His community was known as The Lyrenity founding the religion and dedication in his name. Since he possessed complete knowledge of everything, he grew bored and dissatisfied with the stagnation and complacency of him and his students. So, he resolved in private to write the first narrative which came to be known as The Lyrifica. He did this precisely to experiment and to prevent the potential interference of his students.

As he drew to sleep, black devils flew out of The Lyrifica and unleashed death and chaos to the heavens, slicing the light into ribbons and killing the once immortal residents. Within the fabric of it all, the devils tore an existential vacuum known as the Void much to the horror of all. But when Lyre found out what had happened, he was absolutely delighted. The students flocked to him for guidance only to find he was the source of it all. And here The Lyrenity split into two. The side that committed to Lyre's vision despite the disruption along with the side who sought to murder him and restore the heavens. Those who rebelled succeeded and resolved to strap Lyre to a funeral pyre in his temple where he used to teach. Here, in front of everyone else they set him alight with black flame to which he smiled, closed his eyes and shed a single tear in silence. Thereafter his ashes were scattered and thrown into the void along with who was left of his loyalists. The rebels attempted to find the Lyrifica and put a stop to the madness only to find nothing despairing in wake of the heaven forever lost. More of them were murdered by the devils and the few who survived jumped down to the void with the others.

From Lyre's ashes, the new soil would be sanctified as its own land. Here, Spukland was born. The ghosts of those killed in the heavens remained trapped, their ghost light illuminating the heavens to this day. From there, the story is rife with speculation and contradictions based on the speaker. It is the founding myth of Spukland and the dark counterpart to Apocras' founding myth where Lyre remains the positive guiding teacher to all who follow him.
